How feedback is handled

A short, plain-language explanation of what happens to your data after you submit the CrosshairOne feedback form.

What the form collects

Your email, feedback, optional name, early access preference, and any files you choose to attach. Do not send passwords, identity documents, medical information, or other sensitive data.

Why we collect it

To review your feedback, understand an attached example, answer your question, and — only if you opt in — send you an early access invite.

Who processes submissions

The form is powered by Basin. The service receives, stores, spam-filters, and forwards the submitted data and attachments to the owner of CrosshairOne. Basin has its own privacy policy and data protection information.

Retention and deletion

Retention is determined by the form settings. On Basin's free plan, submissions are retained for up to 30 days; the form owner may delete them sooner. To request a copy or deletion of your feedback, contact CrosshairOne.

Anonymous site analytics

After public launch, the site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate page views, referrers, devices, and loading performance. Cloudflare states that this service does not collect or use visitors' personal data.

This page covers the feedback form only. The CrosshairOne app stores projects locally on your Mac and does not submit them through this form unless you explicitly attach a file.
