02 · FEATURES

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

CrosshairOne keeps things simple: create, save, and display your crosshair over the screen.

Layer-based editor

Dots, lines, and pixel drawings stay fully editable. Move, hide, duplicate, and customize every layer independently.

Non-intrusive overlay

The transparent window never steals focus and lets clicks pass through to the app underneath.

On-screen calibration

The app steps out of the way while your crosshair stays visible. Move it with hotkeys and save the exact position.

Quick Switch

Create a set of favorite presets and cycle forward or back with global hotkeys.

Local library

Search, sorting, favorites, recent projects, and autosave — all on your Mac.

Import and export

Share editable projects in the .crosshairone format — no cloud or account required.
